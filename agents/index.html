Help when you ask. Never behind your back.

AI in WhimzyWriter is optional. When you use it, it proposes and you review. No AI change reaches your manuscript until you accept it, and the words it wrote stay marked.

Choose your model

Set up in Settings, or during the first launch.

Anthropic
Add your Anthropic API key to use Claude models.
OpenAI-compatible services
OpenAI, OpenRouter, or any service with an OpenAI-compatible API and a base URL you enter.
Local models on your Mac
Point WhimzyWriter at Ollama or LM Studio running on your Mac. No key needed.
Apple on-device
On Macs with Apple Intelligence turned on, use Apple’s on-device model. It’s hidden where it isn’t available.

Pick one model for writing and chat and, if you like, a cheaper or local one for summaries and analysis. Either choice can be changed per project. Test Connection checks a provider before you rely on it, and a usage view shows the tokens used per provider and model.

API keys are kept only in your Mac’s Keychain. They’re never shown again after you save them, and never logged or exported.

Chat about your book

In the inspector’s Chat tab.

Ask “Is the pacing too slow here?” and watch the answer stream in. Choose what the AI sees for each message: nothing but the conversation, your selection, the current scene, the current chapter, the whole manuscript, or the relevant passages found by search. A token estimate shows how much you’re sending, and context is never cut without telling you.

  • Keep several conversations per project and come back to them.
  • Stop a reply, retry a failed one, or regenerate and flip between versions.
  • Copy a reply, or insert it at your cursor, where it’s marked as AI-written and one undo removes it.
  • Characters and places you mention from your codex are added as a story bible, so answers stay true to your world.

Writing tools

From the AI menu, the formatting bar, or a right-click.

On a selection

ToolWhat it does
ExpandRewrites the passage longer, with more detail, in the same voice.
Make ConciseTightens the passage while keeping its meaning and voice.
Change ToneRewrites it formal, casual, lyrical, dramatic, playful, dark, or a tone you describe.
Continue WritingAdds one to three paragraphs at the cursor, following what came before.
Custom InstructionRewrites the passage the way you ask.

On a scene, chapter or the whole book

ToolWhat it does
Summarize SceneProposes a two-to-four-sentence summary of what happens.
Generate SynopsisProposes a one-paragraph synopsis for the project.
Check ContinuityReports possible contradictions with a quote, the problem, a suggestion and how serious it is, taking your codex into account.
Split into ScenesProposes where a long scene could be divided, with titles.
Outline from ChaptersWrites an outline of your chapters and their beats to read.
Find Characters & PlacesSuggests codex entries for the people and places in your manuscript.

Tools run in the background. The Tasks tab shows what’s running, lets you cancel or retry, and tells you if something was interrupted when you quit.

Review, then accept

Every AI change is a proposal. Rewrites show inline as a diff, the old words struck through and the new ones highlighted. Larger proposals open a review sheet where you accept or reject each change. After you accept, one undo puts the original back, and structural changes such as splitting a scene can always be undone.

Accepted AI text keeps an authorship mark. Turn on Show AI authorship in Settings to see it underlined like this, and check the status bar for the share of the manuscript the AI wrote.

Semantic search

Off until you turn it on.

Search by meaning finds the right scene even when the words differ, and lets chat pull in passages from twenty chapters back by itself. The search panel switches between Keyword and Meaning, and each result shows the scene, a snippet and how relevant it is.

OptionHow it works
Keyword onlyThe default. Full-text search, with nothing downloaded and nothing sent anywhere.
Apple built-inUses the language model built into macOS and works offline. System assets are downloaded only if you agree.
My AI providerUses the embeddings of an OpenAI-compatible provider you’ve set up, such as OpenAI, Ollama or LM Studio. Your text is sent to that provider for indexing.

The index updates a few seconds after you stop typing, and switching options rebuilds it. A downloadable on-device model is planned as another option.
