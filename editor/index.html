Everything you need to write the book.

WhimzyWriter thinks in projects, chapters and scenes. The editor stays out of your way, saves as you type, and has the formatting a manuscript needs.

Projects

One project is one book.

Create a project with a title, and optionally an author, genre and synopsis. Your projects list shows each book’s word count, estimated page count and when you last worked on it. Project settings hold the defaults the rest of the app uses: the language, the page format used for page estimates, line height, paragraph layout and hyphenation.

Deleting a project always asks first.

Chapters, scenes and snapshots

A tree you can rearrange
Add, rename, delete and reorder chapters and scenes in the sidebar. Drag a scene into another chapter to move it. Every change can be undone.
Counts at a glance
The tree shows word counts for each scene and chapter, and they update as you type.
Named chapter snapshots
Before a big revision, save a snapshot of a chapter with a label. Browse snapshots later, preview them, and compare one with the current text scene by scene.
Restoring keeps a way back
When you restore a snapshot, WhimzyWriter first saves a safety snapshot of what you have now, so restoring never costs you work.

The editor

A centered column of text that follows your Mac’s light or dark appearance.

Character formatting
Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, superscript and subscript, text color, highlight, font and size, from the toolbar, the Format menu or the keyboard.
Paragraph formatting
Alignment, spacing, line height, indents, headings, scene breaks and drop caps.
Autosave and live counts
Your work saves automatically, and the status bar always says whether it has. It shows the scene’s words, paragraphs and estimated pages, plus the project total.
Your view, your way
Zoom from 50% to 300% with ⌘+ and ⌘- or a pinch. Turn on line numbers or typewriter scrolling, or press ⌃⌘F for focus mode with only the page on screen.

Paragraph styles

Named styles such as Body or Chapter Title keep the book consistent. Apply one to a paragraph, and when you change the style, every paragraph that uses it updates.

A project-wide paragraph layout sets how body paragraphs are separated: the classic fiction layout with indented first lines and no gaps, or block paragraphs with space between them. Any scene can override it, which is handy for a letter or an epigraph. You can still nudge the spacing of a single paragraph without making a new style.

Spelling and search

Spellcheck in your book’s language
Uses the Mac’s built-in checker in the project’s language, with optional grammar checking. Tell it to learn your invented names and they stop being flagged in that project only.
Find in the scene
The standard find bar, with case-sensitive, whole-word and regular-expression options.
Search the whole book
Find every scene that mentions “the lighthouse” across text, titles and summaries, and jump to each match. Rename a character everywhere with Replace All, and undo it in one step if you change your mind.
Search by meaning
Turn it on and search for “the scene where she first doubts him” even when the words differ. See semantic search.

Export

File ▸ Export… or ⌘E.

FormatWhat you get
Word (DOCX)Your formatting and named styles, or Standard Manuscript layout: Times New Roman 12 pt, double spaced, 1-inch margins, and a “Surname / Keyword / page” header.
RTFThe same options as DOCX, for apps that prefer Rich Text.
MarkdownHeadings, emphasis and scene breaks, with front matter for the title, author, genre and language.
Plain textJust the words, with an optional header.

Export the entire project, the current chapter, the current scene or a set of chapters, with or without a title page. Files are saved wherever you choose.

Manuscript PDF, EPUB and other formats are planned for a later release.
