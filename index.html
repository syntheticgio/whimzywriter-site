A quiet place to write your novel.

WhimzyWriter is a writing app made for the Mac. Organize your book into chapters and scenes, write on a calm canvas, keep a codex of your world, and ask AI for help only when you want it. AI suggests; you decide what stays.

Coming to the Mac App Store. Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon.

Built for writing a book

More about writing

Chapters and scenes
Each project is one book. Arrange it in a sidebar tree, drag scenes between chapters, and see word counts for every scene and chapter.
A calm, paper-like editor
Full character and paragraph formatting, headings, scene breaks and drop caps. Work saves itself as you type.
Snapshots before big revisions
Save a named version of a chapter, preview it later, and restore it without losing what you have now.
Export when you are ready
Send a whole book, a chapter or a scene to Word (DOCX), RTF, Markdown or plain text, with italics, headings and scene breaks kept.

AI that asks first

More about the AI tools

Your choice of model
Connect Anthropic, any OpenAI-compatible service (including Ollama or LM Studio on your own Mac), or Apple’s on-device model where Apple Intelligence is available. Or use no AI at all.
Chat about your manuscript
Ask about the scene in front of you, or let chat find relevant passages from anywhere in the book.
Rewrites you review
Expand, tighten, change tone or continue a passage. Each result arrives as a diff you accept or reject.
You can see what AI wrote
Accepted AI text keeps an authorship mark. Turn on the underline to see it, and check the AI share in the status bar.

Keep your world straight

More about world building

Switch modes from the toolbar or with ⌘1–⌘5. The Codex holds your characters, places, items, factions, concepts and glossary terms, and shows every scene that mentions them. The Outline board lays out scene cards by chapter with their status. The Timeline tracks when things happen in the story, and Notes keep everything else.

The first few minutes

  1. Say helloA short welcome explains what WhimzyWriter does and what stays on your Mac.
  2. Pick an AI provider, or skip itAdd a key or a local server now, or later in Settings. Everything except the AI tools works without one.
  3. Choose how search worksKeyword search is the default. Search by meaning is opt-in, and nothing is downloaded or sent for indexing unless you choose it.
  4. Start your first projectGive it a title and start writing.
