The book is yours. The app should act like it.

A few rules shape every decision in WhimzyWriter.

Your words stay yours

AI never changes the manuscript silently. Every suggestion is a proposal you review as a diff and accept or reject, and an accepted change can be undone. Text the AI wrote keeps a mark, so you can always see which words are yours and which aren’t.

AI is optional

Writing, organizing, world building, search and export all work without any AI provider. If you do use AI, you choose who provides it: a cloud service with your own key, a local model on your Mac, or Apple’s on-device model. You can use one model for writing and another for summaries.

Your work lives on your Mac

Projects are stored in a database inside the app’s sandboxed container on your Mac, and exports go only where you save them. Text leaves your Mac only when you use an AI feature with a cloud provider, and then only to the provider you chose. API keys stay in your Keychain.

Nothing is downloaded for search without your say-so. Keyword search is the default, and search by meaning is something you turn on.

Made for the Mac

WhimzyWriter is written in Swift with SwiftUI and AppKit, and runs as a single native app with no web view or background servers. It uses the Mac’s own text system, spellchecker, Keychain, menus and keyboard shortcuts, follows light and dark mode, and is built for Apple Silicon.

It will be distributed through the Mac App Store, which handles installs and updates.

Built around the craft

Novels are made of chapters and scenes, drafts and revisions, people and places. WhimzyWriter is organized the same way, so the tools you need are where you expect them: snapshots before a rewrite, a codex for the cast, an outline for the shape of the book, and a timeline for when things happen.
